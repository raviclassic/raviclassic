# 💫 About Me:
Hello, my name is Ravi Chauhan. I am a Film Editor and Post-Production Supervisor with over 7 years of experience in the film industry, currently working with Mukta Arts Ltd.<br><br>My expertise includes film editing, post-production management, sound design, colour correction, DIT, transcoding, media management, and audio-video synchronization. I have experience working with Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve.<br><br>Along with my technical skills, I have a good understanding of filmmaking, including direction and cinematography. I am passionate about visual storytelling, maintaining quality standards, and delivering creative projects efficiently.<br><br>I believe in continuous learning, teamwork, and finding effective creative solutions to every project.
